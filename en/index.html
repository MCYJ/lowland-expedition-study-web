Before you begin

Is this a navigation app?

No. It is a knowledge-study app with a fictional arithmetic worksheet. It does not track your position or guide a real expedition.

Does it award the qualification?

No. The real qualification includes learning evidence and observed practical activity. This app does not replace those requirements.

Is it already available to download?

This site is a coming-soon introduction. No release date or unverified download link is provided.
